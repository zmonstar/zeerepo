# Cinderfella: Instagram Story Reel for AWARE

**Format:** Instagram Story / Reel, vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920), about 36 seconds
**Client:** AWARE (Association of Women for Action and Research), Singapore
**Cause:** Gender equality: everyone valued for who they are, free of gender stereotypes
**Tone:** Cinematic, painterly fairy tale; moves from cold isolation to warm, joyful belonging
**On-screen text:** Opening title and closing call to action only. The story itself is told without words, through pictures, music and sound.

---

## 1. Script

### Opening title (0:00 to 0:03)
> **CINDERFELLA**
> *Everyone deserves a place at the ball*

A music box melody plays in the dark. The title fades in with gold dust, then dissolves into the first scene.

### Act I: Shut out (0:03 to 0:10)
**Scene 1, The hearth (0:03 to 0:07).** In a dim, cold manor kitchen, a gentle young man in patched clothes scrubs a soot-blackened hearth. Two stepbrothers in sharp, expensive coats look down at him, laughing, and one tosses his muddy boots at his feet.
*Sound:* scrubbing brush, crackling embers, muffled mocking laughter.

**Scene 2, The invitation (0:07 to 0:10).** A golden royal invitation glows in candlelight. A stepbrother snatches it from Cinderfella's hands and tears it in two. Cinderfella stands alone at a rain-streaked window, gazing at the distant lit-up palace.
*Sound:* paper tearing, rain on glass, a lonely solo cello.

### Act II: The magic (0:10 to 0:19)
**Scene 3, The fairy godmother (0:10 to 0:14).** A swirl of shimmering light fills the dark kitchen. A warm, kind fairy godmother appears and smiles as if to say *of course you deserve this*. With a wave, his rags spiral into an elegant midnight-blue suit, and glass slippers sparkle on his feet.
*Sound:* rising magical shimmer and chimes; the music swells.

**Scene 4, The ball (0:14 to 0:19).** Grand doors open onto a candlelit ballroom of gold and violet. Guests turn and whisper. The prince crosses the floor and offers his hand. They waltz, and the crowd's surprise melts into smiles.
*Sound:* doors opening, crowd murmur and a gasp; a soaring waltz.

### Act III: Midnight (0:19 to 0:26)
**Scene 5, The stroke of midnight (0:19 to 0:22).** A great clock tower strikes twelve. Cinderfella runs down moonlit palace steps, and one glass slipper is left glinting on the stair.
*Sound:* heavy bell tolls, hurried footsteps; the music falls away.

**Scene 6, The search (0:22 to 0:26).** The prince, holding the glass slipper, stands before a gathered crowd of all kinds of people and raises it high in a gesture of welcome. In a modest cottage, he kneels and the slipper fits Cinderfella perfectly. They share a tearful, joyful look.
*Sound:* a hush, then a single warm piano note as the slipper fits.

### Finale: A kingdom for everyone (0:26 to 0:30)
**Scene 7, The celebration.** Petals and golden confetti fall over a sunlit town square. Cinderfella and the prince, hand in hand, lead a joyful procession of women and men, young and old, dancing together as equals.
*Sound:* cheering crowd, bells, a triumphant full-orchestra finish.

### Call to action and logo (0:30 to 0:36)
The image softens into AWARE purple.
> **Equality shouldn't be a fairy tale.**
> **Make it real. Support AWARE.**
> aware.org.sg

The AWARE logo ("aware / FOR EQUALITY") fades in to close.
*Sound:* the music resolves on a final warm chord, then a soft sparkle.

### Visual language
- **Look:** Painterly, cinematic fairy-tale realism with soft volumetric light and shallow depth of field. The film moves from cold blue-grey shadows (Act I) to warm gold and AWARE violet (Acts II and III, and the finale).
- **Cast:** The same Cinderfella throughout: a kind-faced young man with tousled dark hair. The prince wears a cream and gold coat. The fairy godmother is a warm, silver-haired woman in lilac. The crowds include people of different genders, ages and ethnicities.
- **Type:** One elegant serif for the title and the call to action; the CTA is set in white on AWARE purple.
- **Accessibility:** The text stays inside the Instagram safe area (clear of the top 250 px and bottom 300 px) and is high-contrast.

### Production plan
1. Generate one keyframe image per scene in 9:16, which doubles as the illustrated storyboard.
2. Animate each keyframe into a short clip (image-to-video).
3. Generate the orchestral score (music box, then cello, then a waltz, then a triumphant finish) and the sound effects.
4. Assemble everything in CoDesign: the title card, the clips with cross-fades, the CTA end card with the AWARE logo, and the audio mix.
5. Export an MP4 (H.264, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps).
